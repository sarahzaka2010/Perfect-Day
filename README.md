# Perfect-Day
A website with fun ideas to spend your day with links to provide the best and smooth experience in building your day from Morning, Afternoon and Evening! Starting the day with a bright morning, this website guides you through the day with buttons with links to guide you through different activities you can do. With our main inspiration being 'Perfect Day' from the barbie movie, we wanted it to give cute, whimsy and barbie-vibes that make barbie friendships unique as well as keeping in mind the encore Perfect Day theme. It ends with a quiet night, just like every dazzling morning sun -the sparkling stars also have their time to shine.
